# Firststar

A SaaS where Swiss ice hockey (SIHF) clubs let their fans vote for MVPs of games and season phases, and present their teams and players under the club's own identity.

## Organisation

**Club**:
An SIHF member club that subscribes to the service; the tenant that owns teams, players, branding and votes.
_Avoid_: Organisation, customer, account

**Team**:
One of a club's squads competing in a specific SIHF league or age category (e.g. 2L, U21-A).
_Avoid_: Squad, roster

**Club Admin**:
A club member who manages everything belonging to the club: teams, branding, sponsors and staff.

**Team Manager**:
A club member who runs the games and votes of specific teams only.

**Sponsor**:
A business supporting a club or team, shown on its pages.
_Avoid_: Partner, advertiser

## Competition

**Phase**:
An SIHF season phase a team plays in, e.g. Qualification, Playoffs, Playouts.
_Avoid_: Round, stage

**Game**:
A single scheduled match involving one of the club's teams.
_Avoid_: Match, fixture

**Broadcast Game**:
A game that is streamed or televised, so fans away from the rink may vote on it too.

## Voting

**Fan**:
A person with a single platform-wide account who follows clubs and teams and casts votes.
_Avoid_: User, voter, supporter

**Game MVP Vote**:
The fans' vote, held at the rink (or remotely for a Broadcast Game), on the players of both teams in a single game.

**Phase MVP Vote**:
The fans' vote, held remotely, picking a team's most valuable player over a phase, from the whole team or a club-chosen shortlist.

**Award Format**:
How a Game MVP Vote names its winners: Best Player or Three Stars. Set per team, overridable for a single game.

**Best Player**:
The Award Format naming one winner from each of the two teams in a game.

**Three Stars**:
The Award Format naming a 1st, 2nd and 3rd star from across both teams in a game.

**Star**:
Any award a club's player wins in a Game MVP Vote (Best Player or any of the Three Stars); a team's players are ranked by the stars they collect.
_Avoid_: Point, MVP count

**Prize Sponsor**:
The sponsor providing the prizes for a specific game, featured on its vote and Share Image.

**Share Image**:
A ready-to-post graphic announcing a vote's result in the club's branding.
_Avoid_: Graphic, visual, post
